THE FIRST NOVEL

Project Aurora 371

Built by the Guild. Bound for humanity's destiny.

In 2048, a young geophysicist finds numbers in Earth's own data that shouldn't exist. The world doesn't listen — not at first.

By the time it does, it's too late to fix what's coming. It's not too late to build something else.

Arriving was never the hard part.
Staying human was.

Technology.

The future.

Fabijan D. Svetac writes hard science fiction about technology, human nature, and the future of civilization.

Fascinated by the questions that emerge when technological possibility meets human limitation, his work explores exploration, survival, planetary crisis, and the possibility of a future beyond Earth.

Project Aurora 371 is his first novel.
